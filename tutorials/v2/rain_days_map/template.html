Strongest 60 GHz links this day

Rain rate (60 and 24 GHz links): rolling-standard-deviation wet/dry, constant baseline through wet periods (pycomlink), blackout gaps set to the lowest level, Schleiss et al. (2013) wet-antenna correction, ITU-R P.838 k–R power law. Attenuation = median received level over the 3 days before minus the received level, per sublink (one per direction and radio); dry-period offsets of a few dB remain. 2024-03-23 has 1-minute instantaneous levels (rsl), 2026-03-06 has 6-minute averages (rsl_avg). PWS (v1 record, days up to 2024-07-01 only): Weather Underground stations, 5-minute rain; the link's value is the mean over stations within the radius of the link's midpoint. ASOS: 1-minute rain summed to 5 minutes. Radar: NOAA MRMS hourly QPE from the record's radar days. Times UTC.
